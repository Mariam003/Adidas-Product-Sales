# Adidas-Product-Sales
This project aims to analyze Adidas' product sales performance across products, sales channels, regions, retailer patterns, seasons, and profitability in order to identify the key drivers of revenue, profit, and growth opportunities.

# Business Questions
- Which product categories generate the most revenue, units sold, and profit?
- How does revenue and profit trend over time (monthly & quarterly)?
- Which sales method (Online, In-store, Outlet) contribute most to revenue and profit?
- Which retailers contribute the most to revenue and profit?
- Which regions, states, and cities generate the most revenue and profit?
- How do seasons affect operating profit?
- What is the quarterly performance breakdown?
- How are operating margins and profit per unit performing?
- Which products perform best within each sales channel?
- How do regions contribute to operating profit?

# Executive Summary
The analysis of Adidas’ sales, profit, and regional performance reveals a strong overall commercial outlook, with approximately in $899.9M total revenue, 2M units sold, and $332.1M total operating profit. Performance is driven by a few product categories, strong seasonal peaks, and distinct regional leadership patterns. Retailers also differ significantly in their contribution to both revenue and profit, highlighting clear opportunities for optimization.

- Key Business Metrics
  - Total Revenue: $899.9M
  - Total Units Sold: 2M
  - Total Operating Profit: $332.1M
  - Average Margin: 0.37
  - Profit per Unit: ~$134

- Product Performance Insights
Men’s Street Footwear is the top-performing category, generating approximately $208.83M in revenue and $82.8M in profit. Women's Apparel and Men’s Athletic Footwear also show strong performance with consistent revenue and margin contribution.
Units sold are highest for:
  - Men’s Street Footwear – 593K units
  - Women’s Apparel – 434K units
  - Men’s Athletic Footwear - 436k units
    
- Sales Channel Insights

  - In-store sales lead operating profit at $127.6M, outperforming both Online and Outlet channels.
  - Outlet and Online sales generate comparable profit levels (~$96M–$107M), indicating stable performance but potential for channel-specific optimization.
  - Sales method distribution by units sold:
    - Online: 939,093 units (37.88%)
    - Outlet: 849,778units (34.28%)
    - In-store: 689,990(27.83%)

This means that despite online being the largest contributor to sales volume, in-store continues to deliver the strongest profitability. Strengthening the online profit structure could unlock additional revenue.

- Retailer Insights
  - West Gear contributes the highest revenue of approximately $242M, profit of $86M and largest volume sold of 625K units.
  - Foot Locker also performs strongly with a revenue and profit of  approximately $220M and $81M.
  - Retailers like Walmart and Amazon contribute meaningful volume but significantly lower profit.
  
- Regional & City Performance
  
  - The top region that generates the most profit is the West which was approximately $90M over the years and the least region to generate profit is the Midwest which contributed approximately $53M
  - Top Cities which contributed to over highest revenue over the years was New York which was approximately $13.8M and the least was Huston, contributing approximately $9.8M

- Seasonal & Quarterly Insights
  - Summer delivers the highest operating profit ($95M).
  - Fall and Winter are stable contributors (~$78M–$81M).
  
  - Quarterly Revenue: Q3 is the strongest with approximately $265.3M while Q1 was the weakest with approximately $189.4M. This means Q3 and summer seasons are peak buying periods. Planning inventory, campaigns, and promotions ahead of this window will yield the best returns.

- Trend Analysis: Monthly revenue climbs from early-year lows (Jan–Mar) and peaks in July ($95M) and August ($92M) before falling toward October and rebounding in December.

# Recommendation
- Product Strategy: Expand Men’s Street Footwear and Women’s Apparel inventory for high-demand periods (Summer & Q3).Increase visibility of under performing categories via bundling, pricing adjustments, and promotion.
- Channel Optimization: Invest in online channel profitability → improve operational efficiency, delivery optimization, and targeted digital ads. Maintain strong in-store presence due to its superior profit yield.
- Retailer Strategy: Strengthen partnerships with West Gear and Foot Locker through exclusive releases or volume agreements. Reevaluate margin structures with low-profit retailers (e.g., Amazon).
- Regional Targeting: Prioritize West, Northeast, and South regions for expansion and marketing spend. Localize product assortments for high-performing states like New York, California, and Florida.
- Seasonal Execution: Build inventory and campaigns around Summer/Q3, the strongest season and quarter. Introduce strategic promotions during weaker periods (Q1 and early Spring).
  
# Overall Conclusion
Adidas is performing strongly across product categories, channels, and regions, with footwear driving revenue and in-store sales generating the highest profitability. Strategic focus on top-performing regions, optimizing weaker retail partners, and maximizing seasonal demand will position the company for sustained growth and improved operating efficiency.
